Homecoming Build Competition

Build Week: Tuesday, October 13, 12 p.m. through Sunday, October 18, 11 a.m. ET

This year's theme is Decorate a Golf Cart, and all CMU students, alumni, staff, and faculty are welcome to build. Booth orgs (and your alumni!) are especially welcome.

To be eligible to win prizes, teams must register any time before the end of Build Week. Non-competitive teams may register to get email updates and announcements.

For more information, consult the Homecoming Build Competition rules.
