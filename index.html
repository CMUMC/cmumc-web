The Java edition of Minecraft and an @andrew.cmu.edu or @alumni.cmu.edu account are required to sign in to the CMU Minecraft server. If you need an @alumni.cmu.edu email, follow these instructions.

Homecoming Build Competition

Build Week: Noon Tuesday, Oct. 13, through 11 a.m. Sunday, Oct. 18, ET

This year's theme is Decorate a Golf Cart, and all CMU students, alumni, staff and faculty are welcome to build. Booth orgs (and your alumni!) are especially welcome.

To be eligible to win prizes, teams must register any time before the end of Build Week. Noncompetitive teams may register to get email updates and announcements.

For more information, consult the Homecoming Build Competition rules.
